## Read rules 93-102. Rule 102 was written by round 39, about the builder's error

* **93 — THE PIVOT.** `runCss` emits `scaleX(scale) scaleY(sy) translate(tx,ty)`
  with `transform-origin: 0 0`, so `sy` pivots on the element's **top-left**, `ty`
  is **multiplied by** `sy`, and `translate` lands after both scales. An
  **x-positioned** run pivots on the element's LEFT edge; a **cx-positioned** run
  (`left: cx - w/2`, `width: w/scale`, `text-align: center`) pivots on `cx`. Read
  `runCss`, `markBox`, `hitbox` and `Icon` before converting any measurement into
  a prescription, and **state the pivot you assumed**.
* **100 — vertical text is quantised to 1.0 device px.** Round 39 swept the
  plateau rather than assuming it and put its `ty` at the plateau *centre*, so the
  change survives a ±0.45 px arithmetic error. Do the same.
* **101 — supply a REFUTING measurement**: a landmark, with a tolerance, that can
  be taken before any band is scored, and which comes out wrong if you are wrong.
* **102 — and the two corollaries, both of which cost round 39 real time.** A
  refuting measurement taken *after* the band is an attribution check, not a
  refutation. And **an estimator you wrote yourself is not automatically the more
  trustworthy one**: round 39's disagreed with grade 24 because it merged
  adjacent glyphs (n=9 against n=23). When two estimators of one quantity
  disagree, add a **third that shares no parameter with either** rather than
  adjudicating — there, a single sub-pixel offset fitted over the whole ink
  profile, which has no segmentation to get wrong.
