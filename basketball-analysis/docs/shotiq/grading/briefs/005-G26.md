## Rules 100-103. The last two were written by the last two rounds.

* **100 — vertical text is quantised to ~1.0 device px.** Sweep the plateau and
  put your value at its **centre**, so the prescription survives a ±0.45 px
  arithmetic error. Both recent rounds did this and both landed exactly.
* **101 — supply a REFUTING measurement**: a landmark with a tolerance, takeable
  before any band is scored, that comes out wrong if you are wrong.
* **102 — order matters, and self-authored instruments are not privileged.** A
  refutation taken after the band is an attribution check. And when two
  estimators of one quantity disagree, add a **third sharing no parameter with
  either** rather than adjudicating.
* **103, and it is the one to internalise — TWO ESTIMATORS AGREEING IS NOT TWO
  INDEPENDENT ESTIMATORS.** On `shield` and `gear`, the ink centroid and a
  whole-profile SSD offset **both** said "translate up" and **both were wrong**: a
  height deficit anchored at one end displaces a centroid exactly as a
  translation does. **Paired edge crossings** resolve what those average away —
  edges moving the SAME way is a translation, edges moving OPPOSITELY is a size
  error, one landed and one off decomposes into both. Prefer a crossing (an
  interpolated coverage level) to an envelope (an extreme-value pixel, rule 90).
